What you get

Everything a good IPTV player should do.

Built around how people actually watch: quick to open, steady on match day, and out of the way.

{{icon:grid}}

Multi-view for match day

Watch 2 to 4 channels at once, one with sound. Tap a view for pause, previous channel and next channel. Each view reconnects by itself if its stream drops.

{{icon:layers}}

Cross-playlist view

Put one channel from each of your playlists side by side. Every playlist uses its own account, so providers that allow only one stream still work.

{{icon:refresh}}

Reconnects by itself

A frozen stream is nudged, then restarted, and Tenna waits for the network if you go offline.

{{icon:calendar}}

A real TV guide

Now and Next on every channel, a full guide, and a time-shift for providers whose guide runs early or late.

{{icon:subtitles}}

Audio and subtitles that work

Films with Dolby Digital or DTS sound play with sound, and you can pick the audio language and subtitles from the file.

{{icon:film}}

Movies and series

Continue watching, auto-play the next episode, and skip intros that Tenna learns from your own skips.

{{icon:trophy}}

Live sports

Scores and fixtures by league. Tap a match to open the best channel for it.

{{icon:users}}

Profiles, with a Kids mode

Separate favourites and history for each person, and a Kids profile that hides adult categories behind a PIN.

Getting started

Up and running in a minute.

Install Tenna

Download the installer for Windows or the disk image for your Mac, and open it.

Add your playlist

Enter your provider's Xtream Codes login or paste an M3U link. Tenna comes with no channels of its own.

Start watching

Your library is saved on the computer, so the next launch opens straight away.

Quick answers

Good to know.

Does Tenna come with channels?

No. Tenna is a player only. You add your own Xtream Codes account or M3U playlist from a provider you have the right to use. Tenna has no say over, and no copy of, what is in it. Read the disclaimer.

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no subscription, no ads and no sign-up.

Which Mac file do I download?

Apple menu, then About This Mac. "Chip: Apple M1" (or M2, M3…) means Apple Silicon: get the mac-arm64 file. "Processor: Intel" means the mac-x64 file. The Intel one on an Apple Silicon Mac works but runs far slower.

When will Android and iOS arrive?

They are coming soon. This page will show the download buttons as soon as they are ready.
