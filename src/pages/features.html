Features

Everything Tenna does.

A player for the playlists you already have, made to open fast, stay steady and look good. Tenna provides no channels or content of its own.

{{icon:folder}} Library

Your playlists, kept close.

Add as many playlists as you like and switch between them. Everything is saved on your computer, so Tenna opens straight into your library.

  • {{icon:check}}Xtream Codes and M3U. Enter a login, or paste a playlist link. A login you copied from a message can be filled in for you.
  • {{icon:check}}Saved library. The whole library is stored on the device and shown at once on the next launch, then refreshed quietly in the background on the schedule you choose.
  • {{icon:check}}Profiles. Each person gets their own favourites, favourite categories and watch history. A Kids profile hides adult categories and asks for a PIN to leave it.
  • {{icon:check}}Manage content. Hide, reorder and merge the categories of each playlist, so you only see what you watch.
  • {{icon:check}}Search everything. One search across live TV (including what is on right now), movies and series.
{{icon:tv}} Live TV

Live TV that stays on.

Quick to browse, easy to get around, and built to keep a live stream going when the connection hiccups.

  • {{icon:check}}Channel lists as a grid or a list, with sorting, favourite channels and categories, quality badges and Now/Next on every channel.
  • {{icon:check}}TV guide. Now and Next on every channel and in the player. On iPhone, iPad and Android there is also a full guide for each category: the channels down the side, time across, and a tap on a programme for its details. It works with Xtream accounts and with M3U playlists that name a guide. Manage EPG lets you add a guide address of your own, and shifts a provider's times when its guide runs early or late.
  • {{icon:check}}Reconnects by itself. A frozen or stopped stream is nudged, then restarted with waits between tries. Offline? Tenna waits for the network and carries on. You only see an error, with a Retry button, when it really cannot recover.
  • {{icon:check}}Channel hopping. Previous and next channel with a key on a computer, or a swipe on a phone or tablet.
{{icon:grid}} Multi-view

Watch it all at once.

For match day, news and anything where one screen is not enough.

  • {{icon:check}}2 to 4 channels at once. One has the sound, with a gold border; tap another to move the sound to it, or expand a view to fill the screen.
  • {{icon:check}}Controls on every view. Tap a view for previous channel, pause and next channel. Next and previous move through the same category and skip channels already on screen.
  • {{icon:check}}Each view looks after itself. A view whose stream drops shows "Reconnecting…" and recovers on its own.
  • {{icon:check}}Cross-playlist view. One channel from each of 2 to 4 playlists, side by side. Every playlist is its own account with its own connection, so providers that allow only one stream at a time still work.
{{icon:film}} Movies and series

Pick up where you left off.

Details, resume and the small things that make a series pleasant to binge.

  • {{icon:check}}Details pages with artwork, runtime, genres, ratings and a synopsis, and Continue watching right on Home.
  • {{icon:check}}Series. Seasons and episodes, auto-play next episode, and Skip intro that learns where an intro ends from the times you skip it yourself.
  • {{icon:check}}Good controls. A seek bar that also shows how much is buffered, playback speed, a skip button you can set, and a buffer setting for slow connections.
{{icon:subtitles}} Audio and subtitles

Sound and subtitles, sorted.

Films often carry several languages and special sound formats. Tenna handles them for you.

  • {{icon:check}}Dolby Digital and DTS films play with sound. The desktop apps convert audio that a computer's browser engine cannot decode, on the fly, while the picture is passed through untouched.
  • {{icon:check}}Pick the language. The audio languages and the subtitle tracks stored in a film are listed for you to choose from, and subtitles are shown over the picture.
{{icon:trophy}} Home and sports

A home screen that's useful.

What is new, what is on and what you were watching.

  • {{icon:check}}Home. A banner of trending titles, Continue watching, and rows of new movies, new series and live entertainment, which you can switch off.
  • {{icon:check}}Live sports. Scores and fixtures by league, with a Matches strip and a row for each league. Tap a match to open the best channel for it. Scores come from ESPN's public scoreboard.
  • {{icon:check}}Match alerts before kick-off for the teams you follow (phone and tablet apps).
{{icon:keyboard}} On the desktop

Feels at home on a computer.

A dark title bar, a window that remembers where you left it, and shortcuts for everything.

{{icon:lock}} Private

Nothing to sign up for.

Tenna does not know who you are, and does not try to.

  • {{icon:check}}No accounts, ads or analytics. Your logins and library stay on your device. Read the privacy policy.
  • {{icon:check}}A player only. Tenna ships with no channels and hosts nothing. Read the disclaimer.
