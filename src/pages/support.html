Support

Help and support.

Tenna is a player. It has no channels of its own, so most problems come from the playlist, the connection, or the app. Here is where to start.

Quick fixes

  • {{icon:check}}A channel will not play, or keeps stopping. Tenna reconnects by itself and waits for the network. If it still fails, the channel itself may be down: check it with your provider. Many providers allow only one stream at a time, so close other apps and devices that are watching.
  • {{icon:check}}The TV guide is empty or at the wrong time. Open Settings, Playlists, choose the playlist, then Manage EPG. You can add a guide address of your own there, and shift the times of a provider whose guide runs early or late.
  • {{icon:check}}Adding a playlist did not work. Check the address, username and password with your provider, and that an M3U link opens in a browser.
  • {{icon:check}}The picture or sound is wrong on a film. Try another audio track from the player's menu. On a phone or tablet you can also switch the player in Settings, Video Player.

More answers are in the FAQ and help.

Report a problem

Email {{c:contactEmail}}, or open an issue on GitHub, and tell us:

  • which app you use (iPhone, iPad, Android, Windows or Mac) and its version, which is in Settings, About;
  • your device and system version;
  • what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead;
  • whether it happens on every channel or only one.
{{icon:shield}}

Please never share your playlist login, server address or password, in an email or an issue (issues on GitHub are public). If a screenshot shows them, hide them first.

Content and privacy

Tenna provides no channels, films or series, and is not connected to any provider. For what a playlist contains, or a problem with an account, contact the provider. How Tenna handles data is in the privacy policy, and what it is and is not is in the disclaimer.
